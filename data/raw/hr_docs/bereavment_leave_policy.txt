BEREAVEMENT LEAVE POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Department: Human Resources
Version: 1.3

1. PURPOSE
This policy provides paid time off for employees following the death of a family member or close personal relation, recognising the need for time to grieve, attend services, and manage related affairs.

2. ELIGIBILITY
All employees are eligible from their first day of employment. There is no minimum tenure requirement. Both full-time and part-time employees are covered, with part-time entitlements pro-rated against scheduled hours.

3. ENTITLEMENTS

3.1 Immediate Family
Employees may take up to 5 consecutive working days of fully paid bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member. Immediate family is defined as: spouse or domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or parent-in-law.

3.2 Extended Family and Close Relations
Employees may take 1 working day of fully paid bereavement leave for the death of an extended family member or close personal relation. This includes aunts, uncles, cousins, and individuals with whom the employee had a comparably close personal relationship.

3.3 Additional Unpaid Leave
Where additional time is required, employees may request up to 10 working days of unpaid leave, subject to manager approval. Such requests will not be unreasonably refused.

4. NOTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
Employees should notify their line manager as soon as reasonably possible, by phone, message, or email. Formal HR portal notification may follow within 5 working days of return. Pre-notice is not required given the unpredictable nature of bereavement.

5. SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION
HR may request supporting documentation (such as a service notice or death certificate) only where leave exceeds 5 working days or where a pattern of usage warrants review. Documentation is not required for standard entitlements.

6. RETURN TO WORK
Employees return to their original role with no change to grade, salary, or responsibilities. Phased return arrangements may be agreed with the line manager where helpful.

7. CONTACT
For questions contact hr@company.com or speak with your HR Business Partner. The Employee Assistance Programme is also available for grief counselling support.
