MEDICAL LEAVE — EMPLOYEE FAQ

This page answers the most common questions about medical leave. For the full policy text, see the HR portal. For your specific situation, contact your HR Business Partner.

Q: Who is eligible for medical leave?
A: You become eligible for protected medical leave once you have completed twelve months of employment AND worked at least 1,250 hours during those twelve months. If you don't meet both conditions, you can still request unpaid leave under our discretionary leave policy, but it isn't guaranteed.

Q: How much medical leave can I take?
A: Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid medical leave in any rolling 12-month period. Leave may be taken in a single continuous block or, with manager and HR agreement, intermittently (e.g. for ongoing treatment).

Q: Is medical leave paid?
A: Medical leave under this policy is unpaid. However, you may be eligible for short-term disability insurance benefits during your leave, which typically replace 60% of base salary. You may also use accrued sick leave or vacation days to bridge the gap. Speak to HR before your leave starts to plan this.

Q: What documentation do I need to provide?
A: A certification from a licensed healthcare provider is required. The certification must include the date the condition began, expected duration, and a statement that you are unable to perform the essential functions of your role. HR will provide a standard certification form.

Q: How much notice do I need to give?
A: For foreseeable medical leave (e.g. scheduled surgery), 30 days written notice via the HR portal. For unforeseeable leave (e.g. sudden illness), notify your manager as soon as practicable — same day where possible, within 2 working days at the latest.

Q: What about my health insurance during medical leave?
A: Your health insurance continues uninterrupted during medical leave on the same terms as while actively working. The company continues its contribution; you continue yours, typically by automatic deduction from any short-term disability payments or by direct payment to HR.

Q: Will my job be there when I come back?
A: Yes. Upon return from medical leave you are entitled to return to your original role at the same grade and salary. If your original role is genuinely no longer available due to circumstances unrelated to your leave, a comparable role will be offered.

Q: Can my medical leave be extended beyond 12 weeks?
A: Extensions beyond 12 weeks fall outside this policy and are handled case-by-case, often as a reasonable accommodation under disability law. Contact HR before your initial 12 weeks ends to discuss options.

Q: How does medical leave interact with parental leave?
A: Pregnancy-related medical leave (e.g. recovery from childbirth) is treated as medical leave. Parental leave for bonding with a new child is separate and runs under the parental leave policy. The two can be taken consecutively.

For questions not covered here, contact hr@company.com.
