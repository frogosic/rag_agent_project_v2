SABBATICAL LEAVE POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Department: Human Resources
Version: 1.4

1. PURPOSE
This policy enables long-tenured employees to take an extended period of leave for personal development, study, travel, volunteer work, or rest. Sabbaticals support employee wellbeing and long-term retention.

2. ELIGIBILITY
Employees who have completed three years of continuous full-time employment are eligible to apply. Time spent on parental leave or medical leave counts toward continuous service. Time spent on prior sabbaticals does not.

3. ENTITLEMENTS

3.1 Duration
Employees may take between 4 and 12 weeks of sabbatical leave. Requests longer than 12 weeks require executive approval and are considered on an exceptional basis only.

3.2 Compensation
Sabbatical leave is offered in two forms:
- Up to 4 weeks at 50% of base salary
- Up to 12 weeks fully unpaid

Employees may combine these (e.g. 4 weeks at half pay followed by 8 weeks unpaid). Benefits, including health insurance and pension contributions, continue throughout the sabbatical period.

3.3 Frequency
Eligible employees may take one sabbatical every five years. The five-year period is measured from the end date of the previous sabbatical.

4. NOTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
Employees must submit a sabbatical request to their line manager and HR at least 12 weeks before the intended start of leave. Approval is subject to operational considerations including team coverage and project commitments. Requests will not be unreasonably refused but may be deferred to a later mutually agreeable date.

5. RETURN TO WORK
Employees returning from sabbatical are entitled to return to their original role at the same grade and salary. Where the original role no longer exists due to organisational change, a comparable role at the same grade will be offered.

6. CONTACT
For questions contact hr@company.com or speak with your HR Business Partner. A sabbatical planning guide is available on the HR portal.
