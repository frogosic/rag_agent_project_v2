WELLNESS BENEFITS — EMPLOYEE FAQ

This page covers the wellness benefits available to employees. These are separate from medical leave (see medical leave FAQ) and from health insurance (see your benefits enrollment portal).

Q: Who is eligible for wellness benefits?
A: All full-time employees are eligible from their first day of employment. Part-time employees scheduled for 20 or more hours per week are eligible on a pro-rated basis. Contractors and interns are not covered under this programme.

Q: What's covered under the wellness stipend?
A: Each eligible employee receives an annual wellness stipend of $1,200, reimbursable against qualifying expenses. Qualifying expenses include: gym and fitness studio memberships, fitness equipment for home use up to $400, mental health apps and therapy copays, nutrition counselling, sleep aids and ergonomic equipment, and wellness retreats.

Q: What is NOT covered?
A: The stipend does not cover: cosmetic procedures, supplements or vitamins, weight-loss medications, recreational equipment unrelated to fitness (e.g. ski passes), or expenses already covered by health insurance.

Q: How do I claim reimbursement?
A: Submit receipts via the wellness portal within 60 days of the expense. Reimbursement is processed in the next payroll cycle following approval. Claims older than 60 days are not accepted except in documented exceptional circumstances.

Q: Does the stipend roll over year to year?
A: No. The wellness stipend resets on January 1 each year. Unused amounts are not paid out and do not carry forward. Plan your claims accordingly.

Q: Is the Employee Assistance Programme part of this?
A: The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) is separate and free at point of use. It provides confidential short-term counselling, legal advice, and financial guidance. EAP usage does not draw from your wellness stipend. Contact the EAP directly via the number on the back of your employee ID card.

Q: Are mental health benefits covered?
A: Yes, in three ways: (1) therapy copays from your health insurance can be reimbursed via the wellness stipend; (2) standalone mental health apps (e.g. meditation, CBT-based) are covered up to the stipend limit; (3) the EAP provides up to 8 free counselling sessions per issue per year, separate from the stipend.

Q: How does this differ from medical leave?
A: Wellness benefits are an ongoing benefit while you are actively working. Medical leave is time off when you cannot work due to a health condition. They are unrelated programmes with different eligibility rules and entitlements.

Q: Is the stipend taxable?
A: Reimbursements through the wellness stipend are generally treated as taxable income and appear on your annual wage statement. Consult a tax advisor for your specific situation. The company does not provide tax advice.

For questions not covered here, contact hr@company.com.
